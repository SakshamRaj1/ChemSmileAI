ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Explore Chemistry
through Cheminformatics.

ChemSmileAI is a molecular exploration platform designed to make accessibility to chemical information and RDKit based computations easier to discover, compare and understand with 1 click. Providing search on the basis of Molecule Structure (SMILES), CHEMBL ID, and Molecule Sketching Feature.

HOW IT WORKS

Learn about the workflow of functions and features in a few steps

ChemSmileAI combines search features with sophisticated cheminformatics functions into a single, user-friendly platform that is based on minimum clicks and maximum detailed information and results. This makes it an essential resource for researchers, students, and professionals in the chemical sciences, streamlining the management of chemical information, promoting molecular exploration, and facilitating computational chemistry tasks, thus enhancing both usability and research efficiency.
